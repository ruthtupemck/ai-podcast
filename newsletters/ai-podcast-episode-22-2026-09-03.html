OpenAI Launches GPT-6 “Astra”

OpenAI launched GPT-6, codenamed Astra, on its largest training run yet — over 100,000 GPUs. President Greg Brockman called it a generational leap that may represent the arrival of AGI. But Astra is also the first OpenAI model to cross the company’s own critical threshold for cybersecurity risk. Read more →

Nvidia in Advanced Talks to Acquire Hugging Face

Nvidia is reportedly in advanced talks to acquire Hugging Face, the AI developer platform where developers share and download open models, in a deal worth $12.9 to $14 billion — deepening Nvidia’s control over model distribution, not just chips. Read more →

Cisco Deploys AI Agent to Entire 90,000-Person Workforce

Cisco has rolled out a personalized AI agent called MyAgent to its entire global workforce of roughly 90,000 employees, built on its Circuit AI platform and personalized through memory of each employee’s preferences and past interactions. Read more →

Meta’s Youth-Safety Settlement Clears Path for New AI Products

Meta has settled a lawsuit brought by a coalition of 52 state attorneys general over design harms to minors on Instagram and Facebook. The $18 billion settlement adds usage caps for minors and, analysts say, clears the way for Meta’s AI roadmap. Read more →

California Legislature Passes Roughly Two Dozen AI Bills

California’s legislature closed its 2026 session having passed more than two dozen AI and social-media bills, covering companion-chatbot safety for minors and workplace AI surveillance. Governor Newsom now has until the end of the month to sign or veto each one. Read more →

OpenAI Faces 30 New Lawsuits Over AI-Liability Claims

Thirty new lawsuits were filed against OpenAI and CEO Sam Altman in a San Francisco federal court, bringing the total to 37. The suits allege OpenAI’s own safety team flagged a credible threat eight months in advance and was overruled by leadership. Read more →

Protein Industries Canada Funds AI Farm-Safety Projects

Protein Industries Canada announced $4.9 million in funding for two AI agriculture projects — a disease-risk forecasting tool for pulse crops and a drone-and-LiDAR system for measuring grain, a concrete, revenue-positive deployment in a traditional sector. Read more →
